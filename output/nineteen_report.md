## Reading the result

- **The method works:** it detects the known 17-glyph repeating sequence on f57v (38 matches vs 23 by chance,
  p = 0.002).
- **No period-19 repetition** in any ring text, at word level (9 vs 8.0) or glyph level (907 vs 899).
- **One count of 19** among 203: plant-part labels on f102v2, a pharmacy page. One hit in 203 small counts is
  what chance gives, and it is not on an astronomical page.
- Drawn divisions (sectors, rays, figures) are not in the transcription. Counts noted earlier from the images
  (f67r2: 12 sectors, 12 moon faces, an 8-pointed star; zodiac pages about 30 figures; f68r1: 29 labelled stars)
  include no 19.
- **No sign of a 19-year (Metonic) structure.**
