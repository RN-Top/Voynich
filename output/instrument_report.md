# Are the circular diagrams drawings of a measuring instrument? Results

Pre-registration: `analyses/instrument_prereg.md` (committed before any check). Images: the Yale export
uploaded to the repo (`uploads/yale_ms408_facsimile.pdf`, 214 pages).

| Prediction | Result | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| P1 graduated rim (f67r2 stroke count) | **Cannot be counted** | At the PDF's resolution f67r2 is about 500 px wide and the rim strokes blur together. Needs the full-resolution Yale image. |
| P2 ≥3 pointer lines | **FAIL** | Only one: the dotted line from a star point on f67r2. f67v's four cross arms are symmetric sector-like lines, not pointers. |
| P3 ≥2 exact-centre pivots | **FAIL** | No centre holes or pivot marks found. The holes on f72r and f72v are off-centre (excluded by the pre-registration). |
| P4 labels repeat at the same position | **FAIL** (p = 0.0195, needed < 0.01) | Only 3 non-zodiac diagrams carry positioned labels (f57v 1, f69r 22, f69v 28). 2 same-word/same-position matches vs 0.25 expected; suggestive but not past the bar, and the test has little power. See `instrument_p4_report.md`. |
| P5 sevens and eights | Descriptive only | f67r2 has an 8-pointed star with 8 words around it, but also 12 sectors and 12 faces; zodiac signs have about 30 figures. No 7-or-8 pattern beyond what is listed. |

## Decision (pre-registered rule)

**Not supported.** None of P2–P4 passed, so the result is "Not supported" whatever P1 turns out to be:
even a perfect 360-stroke rim would still need two of P2–P4.

What this does *not* say: it does not rule out that the diagrams were inspired by or copy instrument-style
drawings (12 sectors and ~30 figures per sign fit an astrological calendar). It says the pages do not show the
physical features of a working dial: pointers, a pivot, graduated rims we can count.

Worth a follow-up: the P4 near-miss. The words okeo / okody / ykeey recur across f69r and f69v, two of them at
the same clock sector. A larger set of positioned labels would be needed to test it properly.
