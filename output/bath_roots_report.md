## Reading the result

- **Too few to test, by the pre-registered rule.** Labels rarely use these roots (4 hits among 1,186 label words).
- Descriptive only: 3 of the 4 hits are on bath pages (2.2% of bath-page label words against 0.1% elsewhere), and
  all 3 label **bathing figures** (women), none label pools or tubes: *lchy* (f75v), *olchdy* (f80r), *olshy*
  (f84r). The 4th is *rshy* on the Rosettes foldout.
- If anything, the bath roots lean toward the women rather than the water. With 3 words, this is a lead and nothing
  more.
