## Observation made after looking (not pre-registered)

f67r2's ring of 12 moons alternates almost regularly between **6 red crescents** and **6 gold round discs with faces**.
Clockwise from the top: red, gold, red, gold, gold, red, red, gold, red, gold, red, gold, so 10 of 12 neighbours
differ. Medieval lunar calendars alternate **full (30-day)** and **hollow (29-day)** months, because the lunar month
is about 29.5 days. Six of each gives 6×30 + 6×29 = **354 days, a lunar year**. This was seen after looking, at
modest resolution, so it is a lead and not a result. A check would be whether the ring labels next to the red and
gold moons differ systematically.
