- Spring (March–May): 66 of 89 in tubs (74%). Rest of the year: 2 of 210 (1%).
- Fisher exact one-sided p = 1.1e-43 → **SUPPORTED** at the registered threshold, but the pattern was seen before the full count, so this is a description of the pages rather than an independent confirmation.

Reading: tubs appear only from March to May (with a trace in early June) and never from July to December. This fits the medieval regimen advice that spring is the season for bathing and cleansing. It is also confounded with page style: the spring pages are a separate, coloured group.
