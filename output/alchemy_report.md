# Is the manuscript an alchemical book? Results

Pre-registration: `analyses/alchemy_prereg.md` (committed before looking). Every page of the Yale export
(`2002046 (2).pdf`) was viewed. Sections f75–f116 were viewed at about 100 dpi and the rest as thumbnails.

## A1. Apparatus (needs 2 pages): **FAIL**

- **Fire or flames:** none anywhere.
- **Alembic (head with a beak):** none.
- **Still (vessel → pipe → separate receiver):** none clearly drawn. The jars in the pharmaceutical section
  (f88r, f89v, f99r, f99v, f100r, f101v, f102v) are tall lidded apothecary-style jars standing next to plant
  parts. None has a pipe, spout or receiver.
- **Closest candidates (borderline, not counted):** f78r shows a funnel-like opening, a jointed pipe, a pool,
  then a pipe to a second pool. f81r shows two pools joined by a long tube. f83v shows two large round green
  bodies joined in the middle. All three are pools or bodies holding figures, which the pre-registration
  excludes ("pools or tubes joining figures do not count"). Even if they were counted, A2 would still fail.

## A2. Metal / planet signs (needs 3): **FAIL**

No standard alchemical sign (☉ ☽ ☿ ♀ ♂ ♃ ♄, sulphur, salt, antimony) was seen as a drawn sign or label. The suns
and moons on f67–f68 are faces and discs, which the pre-registration does not count. Limitation: thumbnails can
miss very small marks. A sign hidden inside a line of text would need full-resolution checking.

## Decision (pre-registered rule)

**Not supported:** neither check passed. Even counting the borderline pool pages as apparatus, the result
would only reach "Partly supported".

The drawings show plants, jars of plant parts, bathing figures in linked pools, and astrological circles.
That mix matches the herbal / pharmacy / balneology (healing baths) / astrology books of the 15th century more
closely than alchemical books, which nearly always show furnaces, stills and metal signs. This does not rule
out that the text mentions alchemy. It says that the pictures do not show it.
