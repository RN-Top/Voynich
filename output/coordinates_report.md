# Are the diagram labels measurements (coordinates)?

Pre-registration: `analyses/coordinates_prereg.md`. 12 diagrams with ≥8 positioned labels (298 labels; label codes Lz); 3,842 within-diagram pairs; 10,000 shuffles, seed 20261003.

- Correlation between angular distance and label dissimilarity: **+0.050** (shuffled +0.001); p = 0.0009 → **SUPPORTED**
- Neighbours minus other pairs, mean dissimilarity: -0.010 (shuffled -0.002); p = 0.25

Diagrams: f70v2 (29), f70v1 (15), f71r (15), f71v (15), f72r1 (15), f72r2 (29), f72r3 (30), f72v3 (30), f72v2 (30), f72v1 (30), f73r (30), f73v (30)

## Reading the result

- **Supported, weakly.** On the 12 zodiac wheels (298 labelled figures), labels farther apart around the circle are
  less alike: correlation +0.050 against +0.001 for labels shuffled among positions (p = 0.0009). The simpler
  nearest-neighbour comparison points the same way but does not pass on its own (p = 0.25).
- **This is the pattern measurements would produce:** values that change gradually with position, like degrees or
  coordinates.
- **Alternative explanation:** a scribe labelling the figures in order around the wheel, with habits slowly
  drifting, would produce the same gradient. Writing order cannot be separated from position with this data.
- **The effect is small.** Position explains only a little of how labels differ. If the labels encode position,
  most of each label is something else (a name or a value).
- Only zodiac labels (Lz) have recorded positions. The star labels (Ls) on f67–f68 cannot be tested this way
  without mapping each label to its star in the images.
