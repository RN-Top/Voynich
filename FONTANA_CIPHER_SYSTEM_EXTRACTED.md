## PART 1: THE SPECULUM (CIPHER WHEEL) - PAGES 23-24

### Discovery
Pages 23-24 of the Fontana PDF show the **Speculum** - a rotating disc cipher wheel with multiple concentric rings.

### Structure
- **Center label**: "Speculum" (The Mirror/Wheel)
- **Multiple concentric rings**: Contain plaintext and ciphertext letters
- **Decorative vessel at top**: Indicates steganographic integration (text hidden under images)
- **Ring arrangement**: Inner rings = plaintext, Outer rings = ciphertext variants

### Documented Letters (From Speculum rings)
Reading from visible rings:
- **Inner ring (plaintext)**: A B C D E F G H I K L M N O P Q R S T U V W X Y Z
- **Outer rings**: Show shifted/transformed cipher variants

### Polyalphabetic Method
The Speculum creates multiple alphabet shifts:
- **Position 1**: One complete substitution alphabet
- **Position 2**: Different substitution alphabet
- **Position 3-26**: Additional shift alphabets for each letter position

**Significance**: This is NOT a simple substitution cipher. It's a **polyalphabetic rotating cipher** - more sophisticated than Caesar cipher, similar to Vigenère principle.

| Consonant Category | Letters | Pattern System | Visual Logic |

**Key principle**: Each consonant has unique geometric signature; related letters use mirrored or counted variants

## PART 3: STEGANOGRAPHIC COMPOSITION (Fontana's Signature Technique)

### Method
Regular Latin text + cipher notation integrated on SAME PAGE/LINE rather than separated:

```
Example format (from page 15 of PDF):
"Exemplum textus [CIPHER] cum notis [MORE CIPHER] integrates"
```

### Visual Markers
- **Cipher symbols appear in margins**: Circle+marks in left/right margins
- **Cipher integrated into text**: Geometric shapes between words
- **Decorative elements**: Vessels, diagrams, illustrations contain hidden cipher
- **Hand-drawn paragraph marks**: Author-written (not professional calligrapher), same hand as body text

### Purpose
The steganographic method serves dual purpose:
1. **Hides cipher content** in plain sight (appears to be regular text with decoration)
2. **Teaches cipher** through integrated examples (student learns by reading examples)
