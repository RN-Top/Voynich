## Compositional Signature

All pages share consistent design philosophy:
1. **Layered composition** - text + image deliberately integrated
2. **Cipher systems** - mathematical/astrological notation
3. **Circular organization** - radial arrangements of content
4. **Concealment technique** - information hidden within images
5. **Medical/scientific focus** - pharmaceutical, botanical, technical content
