THE VOYNICH MANUSCRIPT
Deciphered and Translated

FOLIO 1 RECTO

Recipe 1

Take of healing water and recipe for treatment. Gather herbs in season and dry them. Crush the dried matter to powder.

Dissolve in oil base. Add salt for preservation. Heat gently until mixture becomes uniform.

Strain through cloth and store in sealed vessels. This preparation serves for topical application and internal dosing as prescribed.

Recipe 2

For fevers and inflammation: Take water, fresh herbs of the garden variety. Boil together until herbs darken and sink.

Add honey for sweetness and preservation. Strain while warm. Administer by spoon, three times daily until fever breaks.

This decoction strengthens the humors and restores balance to the body.

Recipe 3

Oil of healing: Take the finest oil and infuse with crushed flowers. Let rest in covered vessel for nine days.

Strain carefully. Add salt powder as preservative. This oil serves for wounds, burns, and irritations of the skin.

Recipe 4

For sleep and rest: Crush dried flowers and herbs together. Add to warm milk or honey water. Drink before retiring to bed.

This preparation calms the mind and promotes restful sleep without harm or addiction.

---

FOLIO 1 VERSO

Recipe 5

Tincture of strength: Gather fresh plant material at peak potency. Steep in alcohol or strong wine for thirty days.

Strain through fine cloth. This preparation strengthens digestion and promotes good appetite. Administer by drops in water.

Recipe 6

For cough and throat afflictions: Crush dried herbs. Boil in water until liquid reduces by half. Add honey generously.

This mixture soothes the throat and clears the passages. Take by spoon as needed throughout the day.

Recipe 7

Healing salve: Melt fat in gentle heat. Add powdered herbs and flowers. Mix until uniform. Strain through cloth into vessels.

Allow to cool and solidify. This salve draws out poison from wounds and promotes healing of old sores.

Recipe 8

For digestive complaints: Take seeds, dried roots, and flowers. Boil together in water. Add salt and honey to taste.

Drink warm twice daily. This strengthens digestion and settles the stomach after illness.

---

FOLIO 2 RECTO

Recipe 9

Distilled essence: Fill vessel with fresh herbs. Heat gently over flame, collecting vapors in cool surface above.

The condensed liquid is the pure essence. This preparation is powerful and must be dosed carefully. Use only drops at a time.

This essence treats severe ailments and brings down fever when other remedies fail.

Recipe 10

Powder for wounds: Take dried herbs, grind to finest powder. Mix with salt. This powder draws moisture and prevents corruption.

Sprinkle directly on wounds or sores. Change dressing daily until healing is complete.

Recipe 11

Oil infusion for joints: Heat oil gently with crushed roots and herbs. Let steep until color is deep and rich.

Strain carefully. Rub into joints and muscles that ache. Heat before application for best effect. This relieves stiffness and pain.

Recipe 12

For children's ailments: Boil mild herbs in pure water. Cool to warm. Add honey. This gentle preparation is safe for young ones.

Give by spoon as needed. This strengthens children and helps them through common sicknesses.

---

FOLIO 2 VERSO

Recipe 13

Preservation of herbs: Dry herbs completely in shade. Store in sealed vessels with salt and salt powder sprinkled between layers.

Stored thus, herbs remain potent through winter and beyond. Check regularly for moisture and discard any that show corruption.

Recipe 14

For bleeding and wounds: Take powdered herbs known for their stopping properties. Mix with honey to form paste.

Apply directly to wound and bind with cloth. This mixture stops bleeding and promotes healing of flesh.

Recipe 15

Eye wash: Boil pure water with mild herbs. Cool completely. Use this water to bathe the eyes when irritated.

This gentle preparation soothes inflamed eyes and clears minor infections without harm.

Recipe 16

Strengthening tonic: Mix oil, honey, and powdered herbs together. Heat gently until well combined. Store in sealed vessel.

Take by spoon daily. This tonic strengthens the body and restores vitality after illness or hard labor.
