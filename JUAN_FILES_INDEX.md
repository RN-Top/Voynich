### The Three Layers

**Layer 1: Locked Characters (6)**
- s, a, l, d, o, r → themselves
- Form base pharmaceutical abbreviations

**Layer 2: Digraph Pairs (174)**
- Two-character combinations encoding abbreviations
- Examples: 'ch'→'ol', 'ed'→'da', 'ai'→'in'

**Layer 3: Suffix Markers (6)**
- y → SOL (solution)
- n → PUL (powder)
- m → UNG (ointment)
- e → TIN (tincture)
- i → EXT (extract)
- k → OLE (oil variant)
