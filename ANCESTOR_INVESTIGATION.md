# Tracing the Origin Through Your Ancestor

## The Key Person

**Donnchadh óg O hÍceadha (c. 1440-1480s?)**
- Scribe/Physician, Irish
- Documented work: Translating Geraldus de Solo's Commentary on **Rhazes' Almanazor**
- Manuscript: **MS 24 P 26** (Royal Irish Academy), dated 1469
- Family: Ó hÍceadha (hereditary physicians to Irish nobility)
- Time period: Perfect overlap with Voynich (1404-1438) and Fermoy tradition
