## Who Was Rhazes?

**Al-Razi (864-930 CE), known as Rhazes in Latin Europe**

### Historical Importance:
- Persian physician and alchemist
- Wrote the definitive medical textbooks of the medieval period
- His works: Al-Hawi (Continens), Al-Mansuri (Almanazor), Clinical Observations
- Taught at Baghdad medical school (one of the earliest formal medical institutions)

### Teaching Framework:
Rhazes organized medical knowledge systematically:
- **Description of disease** (what it is)
- **Causation** (why it occurs)
- **Cure/Treatment** (how to treat it)

**Sound familiar?**

### Spread Across Europe:
- Translated to Latin in 12th century (Gerard of Cremona, others)
- Became the standard medical textbook in European universities
- Used in: Salerno, Montpellier, Bologna, Oxford, Prague, Kraków
- Translated to regional languages (Irish, Spanish, German, Czech)
- **Direct connection to your ancestor's work**
