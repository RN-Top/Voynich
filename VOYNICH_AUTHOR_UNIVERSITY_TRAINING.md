### Why Fontana ≠ Definitive Author (Important caveat)

- His known ciphers (substitution) were relatively simple and have been deciphered
- His script differs from Voynichese
- No definitive proof of authorship

**However**: Fontana is evidence that **enciphered medical books by university-trained physicians existed in northern Italy during exactly the right period**.

## Part 4: THE CONNECTION - VOYNICH & RHAZES FRAMEWORK

### Rhazes' Framework: Condition → Cause → Cure

Rhazes (9th century, Baghdad) systematized medical knowledge as:

```
CONDITION (What are the observable symptoms?)
    ↓
CAUSE (What physiological process caused this?)
    ↓
CURE (What therapeutic intervention will restore balance?)
```

This framework was documented in:
- **Al-Hawi fi al-Tibb** (Liber Continens)
- **Al-Mansuri** (Liber Almansoris - especially Book IX)
- **De Variolis et Morbillis** (Systematic disease descriptions)

- Gerard of Cremona (12th century, Latin translation)
