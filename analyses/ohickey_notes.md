# The Ó hÍceadha (O'Hickey) medical family and their manuscripts (background, October 2026)

Erin descends from the O'Hickeys. This file is background, not a test.

- **Who they were:** hereditary physicians to the O'Briens of Thomond (Clare); *icídh* means "healer".
- **Niocól Ó hÍceadha** and Aonghus Ó Callanáin translated a commentary on the *Aphorisms* of Hippocrates into Irish
  in **1403**, the same decades in which the Voynich vellum was made (1404–1438).
- **RIA MS 24 P 26:** written by **Donnchadh óg Ó hÍceadha in 1469**. It contains Irish translations of Gerard de
  Solo's commentary on Rhazes, Pietro d'Argelata's *De chirurgia*, and Arnau de Vilanova's *Regimen sanitatis*.
  It is digitised on Irish Script on Screen: https://www.isos.dias.ie/RIA/RIA_MS_24_P_26.html
- **The shared link with the Voynich is the source tradition,** Latin university medicine (Salerno, Montpellier).
  Arnau de Vilanova taught at Montpellier, in the Catalan/Occitan world that the Voynich zodiac month names also
  point to. There is no evidence of a direct Irish link to the Voynich.

## Possible comparisons

- The *Regimen sanitatis* tradition ties health to seasons, bathing and diet, the same themes as the Voynich
  zodiac and bath sections.
- The page layout and entry structure of these manuscripts could be compared with the Voynich's paragraph and star
  layout.
