# Pre-registration: are the women in tubs on the spring zodiac pages? (medieval bathing regimen)

Date: 2026-10-04. Written before the full count.

## Idea

The medieval *Regimen sanitatis* tradition (translated into Irish by the Ó hÍceadha physicians) recommends bathing and
cleansing in spring. If the Voynich zodiac pages are a health calendar, the figures shown in tubs should concentrate on
the spring signs.

## Data

- **Pages:** the 12 zodiac wheels in `images/yale/`.
- **Months:** taken from the month names written on each page: Pisces "mars", Aries "abril" (2 pages), Taurus "may"
  (2 pages), then Gemini through Sagittarius for June to December.
- **Spring:** March to May, i.e. the 5 pages of Pisces, Aries and Taurus.
- **Counting:** for each page, count the figures, and the figures standing in or holding a tub or barrel.

## Test

- **Statistic:** the share of figures in tubs on spring pages versus the other pages.
- **Test:** Fisher exact test, one-sided. Threshold p < 0.05.

## Disclosure

Close-ups had already shown tubs on Pisces, Aries and Taurus and none on Scorpio and Sagittarius. The pages from
Gemini to Libra had not been looked at closely. A clean confirmation would need those pages, but because the pattern
was seen first, this test is partly post hoc and is reported as such.
