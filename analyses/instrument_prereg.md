# Pre-registration: are the circular diagrams drawings of a measuring instrument?

Committed 2026-10-02, **before** any of the checks below were made. Amend only below the line at the end.

## Hypothesis (from the project author)

The circular "astronomical" diagrams depict a measuring instrument of the astrolabe / volvelle family. These are
dials with graduated rims, sectors and pointers, of the kind used in medieval astrology, medicine and alchemy for
time-reckoning and for placing the planets and signs.

Alternative: the circles are decorative or descriptive (star charts, calendars, cosmological pictures) without
instrument features.

## Background already known before registration (not counted as evidence)

- Zodiac signs carry 29–30 labelled figures each, consistent with 30° per sign. Aries and Taurus are split
  over two pages of 15.
- f67r2: 12 sectors, 12 moon faces, an eight-pointed central star with 8 words around it, and a dotted line
  running out from one star point (ZL3b notes; confirmed in photos).
- f67r2's rim carries a band of repeated stroke groups (IMAGE_NOTES.md).

## Predictions and how each is checked

**P1. Graduated rim.** On f67r2, counting every stroke on the rim band from the full-resolution image, the
total is within ±3% of one of: **360** (degrees), **365 or 366** (days), **354 or 355** (lunar year),
**12 × 30 = 360**, **24 × k** for whole k (hours), or **28 / 29.5 × 12** (lunar months: 336 / 354).
*Alternatively,* the per-sector counts are equal across the 12 sectors (coefficient of variation < 10%).
Any other result counts against P1.

**P2. Pointer elements.** At least 3 of the circular diagrams in the astronomical and cosmological sections
(f67–f73, f85–f86, f57v) show a straight or dotted radial line from the centre to the rim that is not a
sector boundary.

**P3. Centre pivots.** At least 2 circular diagrams show a pin hole, worn spot or drawn pivot mark at the
**exact centre** of the circle. Holes elsewhere, such as f72r's hole beside the bull, do not count.

**P4. Instrument-like labels.** The diagram text (ZL3b locus types C and R) repeats the same label at the same
angular position across different diagrams more often than shuffled positions do (permutation test,
p < 0.01). *Note:* the zodiac day-label test already found no positional repetition across the zodiac
pages (p = 0.76), so P4 will be run on the **non-zodiac** circles only.

**P5. Sevens and eights.** Among the circular diagrams, groups of exactly 7 or 8 elements (rays, sectors,
labels, figures) occur more often than groups of 6 or 9. Counts are made from the transcription and the
photos and listed in full before comparing.

## Decision rule

- **Supported:** P1 passes **and** at least two of P2–P4 pass.
- **Partly supported:** P1 fails or cannot be counted, but at least two of P2–P4 pass.
- **Not supported:** otherwise.

P5 is reported as descriptive only: the counting rule is loose and it is easy to fool yourself with
number patterns.

## Status

P1 needs the full-resolution f67r2 image (Yale viewer image 122 of 214). P2 and P3 need photos of the circular
pages. P4 and P5 can be run from the transcription.
