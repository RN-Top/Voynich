# Pre-registration: is the manuscript an alchemical book?

Committed 2026-10-02, **before** any of the checks below were made. Amend only below the line at the end.

## Hypothesis (from the project author)

The underlying motive of the writing is alchemy: the manuscript records alchemical knowledge or procedures.

## What can be checked without reading the text

Nothing in the script can be read, so the checks below use only what alchemical books of the 14th–16th
centuries nearly always show in their **drawings**: the apparatus and the signs of the seven metals.
Text-only patterns (recipe formulas, repeated openings) are left out because cooking, medical and alchemical
recipes all share them, so they could not single out alchemy.

## Already known before registration (not counted as evidence)

- Earlier contact sheets of the circular pages (f57v, f67–f73, f85–f86) showed no apparatus or metal signs.
- Published descriptions call the pharmaceutical containers jar-like and describe pools joined by tubes in the
  "biological" section. I have not looked at those pages in this project.

## Predictions

**A1. Apparatus.** At least **2 different pages** show one of the following, drawn clearly enough to name:
(a) flames or a fire under or around a vessel; (b) a vessel joined by a pipe or spout to a **separate**
collecting vessel (a still: cucurbit + head + receiver); (c) a vessel head with a downward beak (alembic).
Pools or tubes joining figures do **not** count unless they also meet (a), (b) or (c).

**A2. Metal / planet signs.** At least **3 different** standard alchemical signs (☉ gold/Sun, ☽ silver/Moon,
☿ mercury, ♀ copper/Venus, ♂ iron/Mars, ♃ tin/Jupiter, ♄ lead/Saturn, or the signs for sulphur, salt or
antimony) appear as drawn signs or labels anywhere in the manuscript. Drawings of a sun face or moon face do
not count; the sign itself must appear.

## How the checks are made

Every page of the Yale export (`2002046 (2).pdf`) is viewed. The sections most likely to show apparatus are
examined first: biological (f75–f84), rosettes (f85–f86), pharmaceutical (f87–f102), recipes (f103–f116).
Every candidate is listed with its folio and why it does or does not meet the rule.

## Decision rule

- **Supported:** A1 and A2 both pass.
- **Partly supported:** one of them passes.
- **Not supported:** neither passes.

A "Not supported" result means that the drawings show none of the usual signs of an alchemical book. It cannot
rule out that the text is about alchemy, because the text cannot be read.
