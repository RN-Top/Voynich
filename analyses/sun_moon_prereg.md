# Pre-registration: do the Sun–Moon pages show Moon phases? (image check)

Committed 2026-10-03, **before** the pages are examined for this question. Amend only below the line at the end.

## Idea

Moon phase depends on the Moon's angle from the Sun: together = new, 90° = quarter, opposite = full. A page showing a
Sun and a Moon at a deliberate angle, with the Moon drawn in the matching shape, is a phase diagram.

## Method

Every page in the astronomical and cosmological sections of the Yale PDF (`uploads/yale_ms408_facsimile.pdf`,
f57v and f67–f73, f85–f86) is examined.
- **Sun:** a face with rays, or a face in a circle drawn with rays or labelled as the central luminary.
- **Moon:** a crescent (with or without a face).
- For each page with **both**, the positions are recorded as clock positions around the page centre, along with the
  Moon's drawn shape (thin crescent, broad crescent, full disc with crescent mark).

## Decision

- **Phase table** if at least 3 pages show both a Sun and a Moon at **different** angles, and in at least 3 of them the
  Moon's shape matches its angle (thin crescent near the Sun, fuller when farther).
- **Not a phase table** if fewer than 3 such pages exist, or the shapes do not match.
- The judgement is by eye. Every page and position is listed so it can be checked.
