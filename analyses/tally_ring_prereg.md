# Pre-registration: the tally marks on the outer ring of f67r2 (19-year moon cycle)

Date: 2026-10-04. Written **before** any tally mark is counted.

## What is there

The outer band of f67r2 (the 12-moon page) carries groups of short parallel strokes, like tally marks, between the
red words. At the resolution of `uploads/yale_hires/f67r_spread.jpg` the strokes cannot be counted reliably, so the
count will be made from close-up Yale screenshots.

## Counts recorded

1. G = the number of stroke groups around the ring.
2. S = the total number of strokes.
3. The stroke count of each group, in order, clockwise from the top.

## Predictions (fixed now)

| Idea | Prediction |
|---|---|
| 19-year moon cycle (Metonic) | G = 19, or S = 235 (lunar months in 19 years) |
| Lunar year | S = 354 or 355 (12 alternating full and hollow months) |
| Lunar months | groups of 29 and 30, or 12 groups |
| Lunar mansions | G = 28 |
| Solar year (control) | S = 365 or 366 |

A prediction counts as **met** if the exact count is within ±2 strokes (for S) or exactly equal (for G). There are
many possible targets, so a single hit would be suggestive, not proof.

## Withdrawn (2026-10-04)

Close-up screenshots show that the "tally marks" are ordinary Voynich letters (runs of minim strokes, as in `aiin`
or `aiiin`), not counting marks. There is nothing to count, so this test is withdrawn without being run.
