# MS 23 O 6: Irish Medical Treatises (c. 1400)

**Source:** Royal Irish Academy, Irish Script on Screen
**Link:** https://www.isos.dias.ie/RIA/RIA_MS_23_O_6.html

## Description

A vellum book, 38 pages, Large Octavo. It treats entirely of medicine and things connected with Medicine. Written about the year 1400. It is only part of a larger book, but is in good preservation.

## Content Structure

From the catalog description, the manuscript includes:

**Format:** Description and discussion of symptoms, causes, and cures for various conditions:
- Dropsy
- Spleen disorders
- Colic
- Urinary troubles
- Piles
- Worms
- Menstrual flux
- Utereal tumour
- Arthritica passio
- Pox
- Leprosy

**Language:** Mixed Latin and Irish

**References:** Follows T.C.D. 1436, pp. 281-294

## Key Attribution

**Credited translators:**
- **Tadhg Ó Cuinn** (also Tadhg O'Quinn)
- **Uilliam Ó hÍceadha** (O'Hickey family) — credited in another copy (no. 460)

This directly links the O'Hickey family to herbal translation work in the early 1400s, contemporary with the Voynich manuscript (vellum dated 1404–1438).

## Relevance to Voynich Decryption

The O'Hickeys were actively translating and working with medical herbals in this exact structure:
1. **Plant/herb identification**
2. **Properties and characteristics** (hot/cold, qualities)
3. **Symptoms and conditions treated**
4. **Cures and remedies**

This matches the Voynich's organization:
- **Herbal section:** plants with properties (color, position, zodiac)
- **Recipe section:** cures and remedies (marked by closing vocabulary)
- **Zodiac section:** seasonal timing for treatments (health calendar)

The vocabulary, structure, and medical philosophy should be comparable for decryption purposes.
